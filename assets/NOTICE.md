# About the files in this folder

The 3D models, rigs and animations under `assets/` were generated with [Meshy](https://www.meshy.ai/). The textures and the icon artwork were generated with [Higgsfield](https://higgsfield.ai/). The project's owner made them on paid plans and owns them.

They are published under this repository's licence (see `LICENSE`), for use with this software and its forks.

Both services' terms do not allow their outputs to be used to train AI models. Please do not use these files for that.
